01 / Background & experience

An interdisciplinary professional background

Dawid Karol Kolodziej is a mental health clinician, psychotherapist and counsellor based in Queensland, Australia. He has worked in professional counselling, psychotherapy and psychosocial support since 2013, following early charitable placements that began in 2009.

He works as a Senior Mental Health Clinician at headspace Mackay, operated by North and West Remote Health (NWRH). His practice supports young people and families experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma-related difficulties, autism, ADHD and complex psychosocial challenges. His work brings together evidence-informed intervention, risk assessment and coordination across mental health, disability, education, child protection and youth justice services.

His earlier professional work in Poland included forensic and court-related psychological assessments and specialist opinions in family and criminal matters. His approach emphasises person-centred care, collaborative goals, structured intervention planning and measurable outcomes.

Alongside practice, he is President of the General Board of the Psychopedagogical Association (TPP), Chief Executive Officer of The Psychology Counselling Association (PCA), and Academic Director of the International Counselling Institute (ICI). He contributes to organisational strategy, specialist education, certification frameworks, professional ethics and quality standards.

He has also founded and managed business initiatives, undertaken executive work in the IT sector and served as a city councillor. His interests include forensic and educational psychology, adolescent social maladjustment, family risk and protective factors, counselling and organisational leadership.
